After the match

  • The match screen shows the stay timeline: agreement (view / download PDF), deposit held by Kiki, rent paid to the host 3 days after move-in.
  • If rent was split, the remaining months appear as scheduled payments with their due dates, each payable from the timeline when it comes up.
  • Condition photos: the host adds the "before" set; the guest adds the "after" set when they move out.
  • Move-in and move-out complete automatically on the day.
  • The security deposit returns to the guest 3 days after move-out, unless contested.
